Rain bed method: a single continuous excerpt of stecman 569313 when it fits; for chapter-length beds, non-aligned excerpts are joined with long (≈4 s) irregular crossfades — never a tight loop. Intensity is shaped only by slow, uneven random-walk gain (irregular 1.7–5.3 s checkpoints, cosine interpolation). No grid of synthesized drips.

Production rain/wind/cricket bed levels are **7 dB lower** than preview v2 (linear ≈ 0.447). Spot SFX stay at v2 relative levels.

Horror score (all chapters, remix): a low dark drone bed (szegvari 631650 + LukaCafuka 758478), tension risers into scares, jump-scare stingers, a real heartbeat (TRP 570983) only in tense scenes, heavy breathing, and unintelligible ghost-whisper textures. Supernatural lines (पुकार) are pitched down slightly (about −2 semitones) with a well-like echo. Beds duck to near silence just before scare hits. Voices remain dominant. English phrase whispers were not used.
