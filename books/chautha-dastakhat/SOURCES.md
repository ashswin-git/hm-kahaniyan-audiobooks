# Field-recording and score sources (CC0 1.0 / Public Domain only)

All Freesound items below were checked on their Freesound pages for the licence `Creative Commons 0` / `https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/` before use. HQ MP3 previews were fetched from `cdn.freesound.org`. Wikimedia Commons items were accepted only when `LicenseShortName` / license URL was CC0 or Public Domain. BBC Sound Effects were not used. No CC-BY, CC-BY-SA, or non-commercial items were used.

Beds longer than a source file were extended with long (≈4–7.5 s) irregular equal-power crossfades and offset starts — never a tight rhythmic loop. Ambience beds sit about **−28 to −32 dB** relative to gated voice RMS (lower still under speech). Spot SFX are ducked under dialogue.

The mixer skipped a few collected files that sounded synthetic or mismatched (bathroom vent, motorboat, Nike HQ walla, Lingua Libre pronunciations, synthetic bells). They remain in `raw/sfx/cc0/` for the ledger but were not used in the mix.
